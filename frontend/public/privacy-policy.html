🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

Fitness Smarty collects the following categories of data to provide its services:

  • Account data: Email address, name, and hashed password.
  • Health & fitness data: Weight, height, age, gender, workout logs, meal logs, biometric readings, and fitness goals.
  • FemmeCare data (optional): Menstrual cycle information, pregnancy status, and menopause status. This data is encrypted at the application layer using Fernet symmetric encryption and is never stored in plaintext.
  • Food images: Photos uploaded for AI-based meal analysis. Images are processed and not retained after analysis unless explicitly saved by the user.
  • Usage data: Interaction patterns, feature usage, and aggregated analytics for service improvement.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Providing personalised fitness, nutrition, and wellness guidance
  • AI-powered meal analysis and workout recommendations
  • Progress tracking and trend forecasting
  • Gamification features (streaks, achievements, badges)
  • Service improvement and bug fixing

3. Data Storage & Security

Data is stored in PostgreSQL databases hosted on Neon with TLS encryption in transit. Sensitive health fields (FemmeCare data) are additionally encrypted at the application layer. Access to production databases is restricted to authorised personnel only.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Gemini API: For AI-powered coaching and meal analysis. Food images and text prompts are sent to Google's servers for processing. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Clerk (optional): Authentication provider. See Clerk's Privacy Policy.

5. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

You have the right to:

  • Access: Export all your data in JSON format via the data export feature.
  • Rectification: Update your profile at any time.
  • Erasure: Permanently delete your account and all associated data via the account deletion feature.
  • Portability: Download your data in a machine-readable format.

6. Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. Upon account deletion, all personal data is permanently removed from our databases within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

Fitness Smarty is not intended for users under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Medical Disclaimer

Fitness Smarty provides AI-generated guidance for informational purposes only. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider before making health decisions.

9. Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact: privacy@fitnesssmarty.app
